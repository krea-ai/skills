# Materials Vocabulary for Archviz Prompts

Generic words like "concrete" or "wood" produce generic-looking output. Specific descriptors anchor the model on the right texture, finish, and weathering. This file is a reference vocabulary — pull from it when writing prompts.

## Concrete family

- **Board-formed concrete** — visible wood grain texture from formwork boards, warm grey, slight irregularities at panel joints. "Board-formed concrete with visible wood grain and panel joints, warm grey, soft raking light revealing texture"
- **Polished concrete** — high sheen, mirror-like reflections, light surface scratches if used, cool grey. "Polished concrete floor with soft reflections of the overhead lighting, cool neutral grey, subtle wear patterns"
- **Exposed aggregate concrete** — visible stone aggregate breaking the surface, rough texture, warm grey-tan. "Exposed aggregate concrete with visible river stone, rough warm-grey surface, soft contact shadows"
- **Rammed earth** — horizontal stratification visible, warm sandy tones varying band-to-band, rough natural finish. "Rammed earth wall with visible horizontal striations in warm tans and ochres, slightly rough natural texture"
- **GFRC / fiber concrete** — smooth, refined, slightly waxy finish, often pale cool grey. "Fiber-reinforced concrete panel, smooth refined finish, cool pale grey, fine joint reveals"

## Metal family

- **Brushed brass** — warm gold, directional brush marks visible, satin sheen, tarnishes to deeper warmth over time. "Brushed brass detailing, warm satin gold with visible vertical brush marks, slight tarnish in the recesses"
- **Oxidized / patinated copper** — green-brown irregular patina, matte surface, hand-finished look. "Oxidized copper cladding, green-brown irregular patina, matte surface with hand-finished variation"
- **Blackened steel** — dark grey-black, slight blue or brown undertones from the oxidation process, matte to satin. "Blackened steel structural member, dark satin grey-black with subtle blue undertones, soft directional reflections"
- **Corten / weathering steel** — deep rust orange-brown, mottled finish, drip stains on adjacent surfaces. "Corten steel facade panels, deep rust orange-brown mottled patina, drip staining onto the concrete plinth below"
- **Polished stainless steel** — mirror finish, sharp specular reflections of surroundings. "Polished stainless steel reveal, mirror finish, crisp specular reflections of the sky"
- **Anodized aluminum** — matte to satin depending on finish, color from the anodizing dye, fine grain. "Bronze-anodized aluminum mullions, matte warm bronze, fine vertical grain"

## Wood family

- **Smoked oak** — dark warm brown, visible open grain, satin finish from oiling. "Smoked oak flooring, dark warm brown with prominent open grain, satin oiled finish"
- **White oak with rift cut** — pale honey-tan, very straight tight grain, matte natural finish. "Rift-cut white oak veneer, pale honey-tan with straight tight grain, matte natural finish"
- **Charred shou sugi ban** — deep black with crackled carbon surface, visible underlying grain. "Shou sugi ban cedar cladding, charred deep black with crackled carbon surface, visible underlying grain through the burn"
- **Walnut** — rich chocolate brown, swirled grain pattern, soft satin sheen when oiled. "American walnut paneling, rich chocolate brown with characteristic swirled grain, soft satin sheen"
- **Reclaimed pine** — pale warm tan to grey, visible knots, weathering marks, occasional nail holes. "Reclaimed pine cladding, weathered pale tan-grey, visible knots and nail holes, slightly rough hand-planed finish"

## Stone family

- **Travertine** — pale cream-beige with pitted holes and veining, honed matte finish, warm undertone. "Honed travertine cladding, pale cream-beige with characteristic pitted holes and soft horizontal veining"
- **Marble (Carrara)** — pale grey-white with crisp grey veining, polished high gloss or honed matte. "Honed Carrara marble vanity, pale grey-white with crisp directional grey veining, matte finish"
- **Marble (Calacatta)** — bright white with dramatic gold-veined veining. "Polished Calacatta marble feature wall, bright white with dramatic gold and brown veining, high gloss reflecting the lighting"
- **Slate** — dark blue-grey to charcoal, cleft natural surface with subtle irregularities. "Cleft slate flooring, dark blue-grey with natural surface irregularities, soft matte finish"
- **Limestone** — pale cream to beige, fine even grain, soft warm look. "Bath stone limestone facade, pale warm cream, fine even grain, soft matte natural finish"

## Glass family

- **Low-iron clear glass** — near-perfectly clear, no green tint, sharp reflections. "Low-iron clear glazing, perfectly transparent with crisp sky reflections"
- **Fritted glass** — printed dot or line pattern, partial opacity. "Custom fritted glass with linear dot gradient, partial opacity reducing solar gain while preserving views"
- **Channel glass** — translucent, fluted vertical bands, soft diffused light passage. "U-channel cast glass facade, translucent with vertical fluting, soft glowing interior light passing through at dusk"
- **Mirror glass** — full reflective coating, no transparency. "Reflective mirror-glass facade, full specular reflection of the sky and surrounding context"

## Plaster / masonry / tile

- **Smooth lime plaster** — slightly variable hand-applied finish, warm chalk-white to soft sand tones, matte. "Smooth lime plaster wall, slightly variable hand-applied finish, warm chalk-white, matte"
- **Tadelakt plaster** — polished waterproof Moroccan plaster with subtle sheen, deep saturated earth tones. "Tadelakt plaster wall in deep terracotta, polished waxed finish with subtle sheen"
- **Roman brick** — long thin proportions, warm red-to-brown range, slight color variation, soft mortar joints. "Roman brick facade, long thin proportions, warm red-brown range with soft variation, recessed mortar joints"
- **Cleft bluestone / flagstone** — irregular natural stone, blue-grey to charcoal, cleft surface. "Cleft bluestone paving, irregular natural stone in cool blue-grey, soft matte finish"
- **Terrazzo** — composite stone surface with visible chip aggregate, polished finish. "Polished terrazzo floor with mixed cream and warm aggregate chips, soft satin sheen"
- **Ceramic tile (handmade zellige)** — irregular hand-made tile with variation in color and surface, glazed sheen. "Handmade zellige tile facade, irregular pale green glaze with surface variation catching the light"
- **Subway tile (machine glazed)** — uniform rectangular tile, even glaze, crisp grout lines. "Glazed white subway tile, uniform machine finish, crisp dark grout lines"

## Paving / ground

- **Granite cobbles** — irregular cubic stones, mixed grey range, weathered tops. "Granite setts paving, mixed grey weathered tops, irregular joint pattern"
- **Asphalt** — dark grey-black, slight aggregate texture, occasional weathering streaks. "Aged asphalt paving, dark grey-black with subtle aggregate visible, slight weathering"
- **Decomposed granite** — pale tan loose aggregate, slightly compacted, foot-traffic patina. "Decomposed granite path, pale warm tan, slightly compacted with light foot-traffic patina"

## Planting / landscape

- **Mature oak (deciduous)** — large dappled canopy, broad spreading habit. "Mature oak canopy casting dappled afternoon shadow over the entrance plaza"
- **Olive grove** — silver-grey foliage, gnarled trunks, sparse canopy. "Established olive trees with silver-grey foliage and gnarled trunks framing the entrance"
- **Ornamental grasses (Miscanthus / Pennisetum)** — feathery vertical mass, warm tones in autumn. "Ornamental grasses, feathery vertical mass in warm autumn tones, soft movement"
- **Green roof / sedum mat** — low textured ground-cover, varied warm tones, rosette forms visible. "Sedum green roof, varied rosettes in warm tan-green-russet tones, low texture"

## Fabric / soft surfaces

- **Wool boucle** — textured looped surface, soft warmth, neutral oatmeal or cream tones. "Wool boucle upholstery, textured looped surface, soft cream tone"
- **Linen** — natural fiber weave, slight wrinkles, matte. "Belgian linen drapery, natural fiber weave with soft wrinkles, matte finish, warm white"
- **Velvet** — directional sheen depending on nap direction, rich color depth. "Cotton velvet upholstery, rich emerald green with directional sheen, soft saturated color"
- **Leather (oxblood / cognac / black)** — natural grain visible, satin sheen, age-worn patina if used. "Oxblood leather banquette upholstery, visible natural grain, satin sheen, slight patina on the edges"
- **Wool carpet** — soft fiber texture, matte, holds shadow detail. "Wool carpet in oatmeal, soft pile texture, matte, holding contact shadows beneath furniture"
- **Acoustic felt panels** — flat matte fiber surface, often warm muted tones, sound-absorbing. "Acoustic felt panels in warm muted ochre, flat matte fiber surface, soft directional shadows from the side light"

## How to use this list in a prompt

Don't dump every descriptor. Pick the 2-4 that matter most for the brief and let the model handle the rest.

**Bad** (vague): "concrete and wood interior"

**Good** (anchored): "interior with board-formed concrete walls and rift-cut white oak flooring"

**Better** (specific to mood): "interior with board-formed concrete walls showing visible wood grain and panel joints, rift-cut white oak flooring in pale honey-tan with matte oiled finish, warm midday daylight"

Always pair material descriptors with at least one lighting cue — materials read differently under different light, and models that don't get the lighting will misread the material. See `lighting.md`.
