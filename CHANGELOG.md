# Changelog

## 0.7.0

- Rebuilt `krea-animation` around Seedance 2 as its default engine and around cinematic shot craft as its primary spine. `SKILL.md` now opens on Seedance (why it wins: physical plausibility under camera movement, printed-text and mark survival, real cuts inside one generation, four-channel reference control), states the house register (maximal, kinetic and dramatic — cut hard, open inside the subject, reveal it whole last), and carries 16 hard rules including a standing requirement to answer with a staged cut sequence rather than one continuous move, to open inside the subject and reveal it whole only on the final beat, to jump scale/angle/light on every cut, and to gate the start image with vision because it is the quality ceiling.
- Added `krea-animation/references/seedance-prompt-architecture.md`: the nine load-bearing prompt blocks (format, references, world, motion split, shots, light, transitions, audio, constraints tail), the skeleton, per-block rules, the beat budget, scene-detection cut verification, a symptom → cause → prompt-fix failure table, and one-change-at-a-time retake prompting. Resolves the long-standing pace contradiction explicitly: the banned-adverb guardrail exists because `slow`/`gently`/`softly` produce speed-ramped footage, so elegant slowness is written as a quantified camera move (distance + duration + constant rate) paired with stated realtime physics — commanded stillness, not accidental slow-motion.
- Added `krea-animation/references/cinematic-craft.md`: six laws of dramatic motion (spend on cuts not clutter, darkness as material, short beats with controlled motion inside them, nothing wobbles, light as an event per beat, the whole subject arrives last and lands dead still), a component-by-component breakdown of the premium launch-film look, lens and light-setup language, shadow and focus written as verbs, material behaviour table, composition, tempo, grade, atmosphere dosage, and an anti-patterns checklist.
- Added `krea-animation/references/reveal-recipes.md`: 24 named effects grouped into light/shadow reveals, focus and optical reveals, camera-driven reveals, object and matter events, and frame-level grammar — each with the prompt language, what to attach, and how it fails — plus an intent → recipe selection table and proven combinations.
- Added `krea-animation/references/dimensional-motion.md`: air, orbit and depth work — the suspension lock and contact-shadow behaviour that make levitation read as deliberate rather than floaty, controlled rise, multi-object mid-air choreography with depth and occlusion clauses, camera orbit vs object turntable (and when to prefer each), exploded views and reassembly with the shape/scale locks that stop morphing, cutaways, macro dives with named destinations and focus coupled to the move, surface traverses, and 3D/CGI/architectural camera work gated on a geometry lock.
- Added `krea-animation/references/logo-and-mark-motion.md`: the three-place text lock, eight mark treatments led by per-element ignition and the macro cut chain, an avoid table drawn on the building-versus-lighting line (assembling, draw-on, morphing, liquid-formed logos and tumbling 3D marks are out; per-letter illumination and millimetre lifts are in), per-glyph vision QA on first/middle/last frames, and the composite-in-post fallback.
- Added `krea-animation/references/seedance-routing.md` (replaces `krea-model-strategy.md`): Seedance as the default engine, variant selection and the block-cheap/deliver-expensive escalation, the five required live checks per session, expected schema shape, mutually exclusive media paths, the short list of when not to use Seedance, and cost discipline.
- Workflows: added `workflows/cinematic-shot.md` (replaces `still-to-motion.md`) as the skill's default entry point for one superb shot — vision gate, move selection, nine-block prompt, block on the fast variant, frame-level judgement with scene detection, one-change retakes, deliver on the quality variant — plus a defaults table covering beat count, beat length and per-cut relighting. Added `workflows/reveal-sequence.md` for multi-beat reveal films with an explicit choice between one multi-beat generation (default ≤15s) and chained assembled shots, beat-sheet-first planning, and contact-sheet QA.
- `shotlist-to-sequence.md`, `series-from-scratch.md`, `studio-shot-production.md`, and `retakes-and-delivery.md` now resolve models through `seedance-routing.md`, write prompts through the block architecture, and inspect last frames plus scene detection before delivery. Removed `references/motion-prompting.md` (superseded by the prompt architecture).
- Reoriented the whole skill from restraint to **maximal cut-driven cinema**. The house register was "one object, one light, one move — the kind of shot where almost nothing happens", and the six laws led with subtraction, slow camera, and "light does the work, not motion" — which is a specification for a highlight drifting over a static subject, and that is what it produced. The default answer is now a staged cut sequence: four or more beats, macro detail first, scale/angle/key changing on every cut, and the whole subject revealed only on the final beat.
- Added `krea-animation/references/luxury-showcase.md`: environment, material and palette for objects that must read as desirable. The tension principle (pair the ground against the object's material) with a pairing table and two settings worth naming outright, place-don't-present staging, one dominant neutral plus a single warm accent capped under five percent of frame, environment grade as a build device (high-key to true black while the object stays fixed — the one case where showing the whole object early is correct, because the transformation is the reveal), luxury-house references translated into craft terms rather than named as imitation targets, and the approved-still-first path since asking the video model to invent a room around a product photo warps the product.
- Blocked the cheap motion-graphics defaults. A generated logo sting came back as white bars sliding across flat artwork with zero real cuts, because nothing distinguished a flat 2D vector mark from a physical object: a flat mark has no material to rake light across and no depth to push into, so macro beats became a zoom into flat colour and the lighting recipes degenerated into sliding bars. `logo-and-mark-motion.md` now gates on flat vs dimensional before any treatment is chosen, Slat Crawl and the light/shadow wipes are marked physical-subjects-only, and an explicit cheap list (streaks, shine sweeps, glints, sliding bars and stripes, swipe transitions, gradient sweeps, bokeh, scan lines, glow pulses) is banned in the anti-patterns checklist and in every worked prompt tail.
- Fixed the loop the maximal rewrite briefly shipped: the worked logo prompt revealed the full lockup at 3.6s, cut back to a macro detail, then showed the lockup again — a full/detail/full pattern that reads as a repeat and invites the model to actually repeat, on top of asking for five beats against a four-beat ceiling. Beats are now monotonic, the payoff appears exactly once, and both worked prompts carry a tail clause forbidding replays. `seedance-prompt-architecture.md` adds the rule that unstaged duration gets filled with a degraded replay of an earlier beat, so buy only the seconds you have staged.
- Output verification now leads with a contact sheet rather than first/middle/last frame extraction, which structurally cannot see a clip that resolves early and replays — every sampled frame of a looped piece looks correct. The checks name the failure: does the whole subject appear exactly once, does the timeline progress or alternate, is any beat a run of near-identical cells.
- Added `krea-animation/references/cut-architecture.md`: the standard five-beat build, the three rules that make cuts read (change scale and angle, relight every beat, one physical event per beat), the recurring structures (staccato detail chain, blur-out reveal, angle slam, sequential element ignition), the honest beat ceiling (~4 staged beats per generation) and the trim-and-assemble path for true one-second cutting, since the duration floor is 4s.
- `scripts/`, `assets/templates/` and `references/project-structure.md` stay as-is for filesystem agents (Claude Code, Codex), which can execute them. They are excluded from the hosted copy of the skill, which has no persistent working directory.
- Every internal link is now a skill-root-relative path (`references/x.md`, `workflows/y.md`) instead of `../references/x.md` or a bare filename, so the same doc resolves under both filesystem agents and hosted runtimes that address skill files by manifest path.
- Evals: added 2 scenarios (Seedance-first cinematic reveal routing, levitation and macro-dive craft); refreshed scenarios 25 and 39 to name the cinematic-shot workflow instead of the removed still-to-motion one. Dropped the effects-library scenario along with the feature.
- Bumped synced package, plugin, marketplace, codex/cursor manifest, and skill versions to `0.7.0`.
