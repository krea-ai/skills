## 0.6.1

- Rewrote `krea-marketing/workflows/cinematic-product-ad.md` around the live-verified primary path: ONE structured multi-shot timeline prompt on an audio-capable video model (Seedance-2) instead of stills-first assembly. A/B-verified in production: fully-staged shots (time range + shot size + lens + one move + named transition) land as REAL cuts inside a single generation — scene detection confirms them — while unstaged "HARD CUT" lists render as soft morphs. The recipe now gates the product image first (the start image is the quality ceiling: a cropped video frame garbled the logo in every generation regardless of prompt; a clean retailer-grade shot held dense bilingual label text through six beats), plans a beat sheet of 4-6 beats all <=2.5s (the model commits ~3 strong beats on its own; a long late hold makes the back half coast into one slow rotation — verified 15s failure), and writes the prompt with a load-bearing architecture: Format header, inline `@Image1` role + text-lock list, one consistent world, motion split, per-shot staging, per-cut audio hits, and a constraints tail that fights late-generation drift. Length must be added shots, never added adjectives (motion verbs with physical consequences, not image-model vocabulary). Output QA runs scene detection on the RESULT to prove the cuts landed, plus a full-duration contact sheet with first-AND-last-frame text checks; best-of-2/3 seeds for hero deliverables. Stills-first survives as the surgical retake/fallback path.
- `krea-generate/references/models/seedance-2.md`: added the beat-budget rule for multi-shot timelines (~3 strong beats per generation; <=2.5s per beat + "no long static holds" or the back half coasts; unstaged cuts morph — verify with output scene detection) and a live-verified One-Shot Product Commercial template with the full staged-timeline skeleton.
